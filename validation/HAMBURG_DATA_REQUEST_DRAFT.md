Subject: Access to CEDVAL and Michelstadt flow/turbulence measurements for dtcc-qes validation

Dear EWTL team,

We are preparing an independent validation campaign for dtcc-qes, an urban
wind modelling code. Our focus is mean wind and turbulence near pedestrian
height, together with roof shear layers and flow through urban passages.

We would like access to suitable CEDVAL/CEDVAL-LES and Michelstadt datasets,
including the measurement archive passwords and applicable use conditions.
Our intended use is to reserve eligible measurements for independent
confirmation after development on other datasets.

Could you advise which releases provide matched inlet mean velocity and
three component variances, and local U/V/W and Reynolds stresses at near-ground
and at least two higher levels? We are also interested in the boundary-layer
reference cases and how their conditions map to the urban experiments.

For reproducibility, we would appreciate geometry/roof definitions, model scale,
coordinate and wind-direction conventions, roughness and inflow conditions,
measurement coordinates/heights, averaging methods and duration, instrument
uncertainty, covariance sign conventions, and quality or missing-data flags.
Please identify quantities that were not measured rather than inferred.

Please also confirm citation requirements and whether we may publish derived
validation figures, summary metrics and scripts, and whether any numeric subset
can accompany a reproducibility archive. We will review and return any required
data-policy agreement through our project lead.

Kind regards,
[Project lead name and affiliation to be supplied before sending]
